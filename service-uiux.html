Our Research Methods

Every method we run falls into one of two lenses — what people say and feel, or what the numbers prove at scale. We use both, together.

Qualitative

Understanding why

Small-sample, high-depth sessions that surface motivations and friction users can't always put into words.

User Interviews

One-on-one conversations that uncover needs, pain points, and the mental models driving behavior.

Usability Testing

Moderated and unmoderated task-based sessions with real users to validate design hypotheses.

Card Sorting

User-led information architecture exercises to find the content groupings people actually expect.

Quantitative

Measuring how much

Large-sample, statistically grounded research that tells us which patterns actually hold at scale.

Analytics Review

Heatmaps, session recordings, and funnel data, mined to pinpoint exactly where friction occurs.

A/B Testing

Statistical testing of design variants against real traffic to find the highest-performing solution.

Competitive Benchmarking

Scoring industry leaders against measurable UX KPIs to find the gap worth closing.
