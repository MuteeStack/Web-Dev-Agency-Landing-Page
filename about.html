How We Work

Six principles that define how we approach every project, every time.

01

Strategy First

Every pixel we place is backed by research, data, and a deep understanding of your business goals and target audience.

02

Pixel-Perfect

We obsess over the details — micro-interactions, spacing, and typography that elevate good to extraordinary.

03

Rapid Iteration

We ship fast, learn faster. Agile sprints keep your project moving forward without sacrificing quality.

04

Global Perspective

Having served clients across 30+ countries, we bring a culturally rich, globally-informed design perspective to every project we touch.

05

True Partnership

We embed ourselves in your team. Your success is our success — we're in it for the long haul.

06

Forward Thinking

We stay on the cutting edge of WebGL, AI-driven UX, and emerging design patterns to future-proof everything we build.

Our Journey

From a two-person startup to a globally recognized digital studio.

2013
The Studio is Born
Forma is founded by a duo of rogue designers with a laptop, a big idea, and an obsession with the web.
2016
First Major Award
We win our first Site of the Day for a campaign microsite, putting Forma on the global design map.
